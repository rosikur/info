Pixl Hackclub

Pixl is another YSWS but this time you are allowed to create ANYTHING hacker related! Wanna build a Flashlight?! Just do it track your time and get pixels which you can redeem later in the shop. My Latest Project for Pixl was even making a Flashlight. "Well whats so cool about a flashlight" you might ask but its so cool, for this design i Used 3 Li-Ion cells in Series with a Step up to get the appropiate Wattage , we also used a BMS and a Li-Ion Charger and most Importantly the LED we Used a High Power High CRI LED with 18 Watts and 56 Volts. I also Incorporated a Heatsik to make the led not desolder itself. The Idea of it is that you can use any big enough Tube Like object like a Pringels can or a Monster Can.
